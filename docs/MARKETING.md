# The POTIFY Chronicles marketing and sales plan

**Owner.** Chance owns the public release and approves campaign copy.

**Audience.** Serial-fiction and illustrated-book readers. **Offer.** Give readers a clean, shareable entry to the story behind POTIFY.

| Proposed timing | Public material | Desired outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Days 1–3 | Publish a founder reading from the approved excerpt and invite readers into Book I. | Give the audience one verifiable reason to try the project. |
| Days 4–10 | Share the reviewed demo or sample through the project page and one relevant creator or community channel. | Read the opening pages. |
| Days 11–28 | Publish a build note showing what the trial revealed and one change made from feedback. | 12 engaged readers. |

### Sales test

Present a proposed $12 digital collected edition to five engaged readers after the contents are known. Seek two paid orders before discussing a physical run. Ask readers whether they want a collected print volume. Start a paid edition only after production cost and rights are known.

### Measurement and decision

Use `chronicles-first-public-build` for the pilot. Record a 28-day prelaunch baseline where available, then observe each participant for seven days after their first visit. Current audience, conversion and net revenue baselines are unknown. The numbers above are proposed targets. Distinguish visits, qualified actions, buyer inquiries and paid receipts. Keep private identifiers in the authorized reporting system. If the outcome cannot be attributed, label it unknown.

Proposed pilot budget is $0 for distribution and two founder hours for the first demonstration. At day 7, inspect the biggest point of friction. At day 28, expand only if the target action was measured and the sales test produced identifiable demand. Report fulfilled sales net of refunds and direct costs. A product serving trust or internal operations may have no direct sales at all.

**Prerequisite for external promotion.** This edition contains the public opening. Unreleased Book II and branching manuscripts stay with their author. Draft copy and placements go through the existing approval process.
