The Curator

Cultivator’s Note

Every cycle leaves a record. Some records are written in soil. Others are written in memory. This one was written in observation.

The entries that follow were never intended to become a narrative. They began as simple notes — brief records of changes within a controlled environment. Light levels adjusted. Soil conditions measured. Growth patterns observed. Nothing unusual for a cultivator.

Introduction

Growth rarely announces itself. It begins quietly.

A seed placed into soil beneath a light.

To an outside observer, the room appears empty. The trays are still. The air is silent. The lights hum above soil that seems unchanged.

But beneath the surface, the first processes begin immediately. Moisture shifts. Roots search. Energy wakes.

This is how every cycle begins.

Some follow the Chronicle to watch the plants grow. Others begin to understand the system behind them. This is the first cycle.
